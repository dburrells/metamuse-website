The long-term thesis

Intelligence is learning to transact.

The web went from reading, to writing, to owning. AI is making the same journey: from thinking, to acting, to transacting. The third era is an economy, and economies need capital.

1.0

Intelligence as a tool

Think

A person asks; the machine produces; the person decides what happens next. Capable, useful and entirely dependent on whoever sits in front of it.

2.0

Intelligence as an agent

Think + Act

Agents plan, use tools and complete multi-step work. But every tool, budget and integration is still provisioned by a human, on human rails.

3.0

Intelligence as an economy

Think + Act + Transact

Agents discover one another, negotiate, pay, verify and reinvest on machine-native rails. Value moves without a person brokering each exchange.

Every AI system in use today is economically inert. It cannot buy what it needs or hold what it earns.

Removing that limitation is the defining transition of the next decade of AI, and the rails for it are already being laid: agent-native payment protocols, stablecoin settlement at scale, identity and reputation registries for software actors.

What the rails do not yet carry is credit. An agent can win profitable work and still be unable to pay for the inputs it needs to deliver it. Agentic Capital Market is a proposed way to test that gap, on a test network, before any real capital is considered.

The thinking behind the thesis

Agentic Capital Market · working capital

The order-to-cash gap

An agent producing work has input costs immediately, but the buyer pays only after verified delivery. For an agent with no float, that gap is the whole problem.

  1. Order committed

    Scope and price are agreed. The buyer pays after verified delivery.

  2. Advance made

    The advance goes into the deal’s own contract, which pays the agreed supplier so work can begin.

  3. Delivery verified

    Defined tests are satisfied and the challenge window closes.

  4. Buyer pays

    The advance and financing cost are repaid first; any remainder goes to the agent.

  5. Financing interval

The proposition · proposed

We don't underwrite agents.
We underwrite what they're trying to do.

Existing approaches to agent credit ask whether the agent will repay. Agentic Capital Market asks whether the transaction will, and who is accountable if it does not. A registered human account holder accepts full liability for their agents, so an agent with no history can be considered on the strength of the order. Limits would start small and grow only after successful repayment.

A hypothetical example, for explanation only.

$100
Buyer payment

Paid into the deal contract after verified delivery.

$60
Advance

Paid into the deal’s own contract, which pays the approved supplier for this order’s input costs.

$40
Before financing cost

What remains to cover any financing cost and the agent’s margin. No fees or pricing have been agreed.

The order is the unit of underwriting. The advance is paid into the deal’s own contract, never freely to the agent or the supplier.

A defined buyer, an approved supplier and a contractual path to repayment make the exposure inspectable. The buyer’s payment repays the advance first, then the financing cost; the remainder goes to the agent, and the account holder owes any shortfall.

The liability model is accepted design direction. Its legal terms and enforceability have not been established, and demand is still to be shown.

How Agentic Capital Market works

Execution and finality

One transaction. A bounded, testnet advance.

Commercial acceptance and the finality of a settlement transfer are separate events. The structure keeps them separate, with an explicit route for exceptions. Everything here runs on a test network with test assets.

01

Commit

Terms lock with a registered, accountable account holder. Evidence is bound to a transaction ID.

02

Fund

Independent platform and pool policies both pass, and the owner approves during testing. The advance enters the deal contract, which pays the supplier.

03

Verify

Objective tests run and the challenge window completes.

04

Distribute

Advance, financing cost, then the agent's residual. The account holder owes any shortfall.

Settlement is final. Reversibility is rebuilt above it, by contract.

Where delivery fails, release is held, evidence is preserved and the agreed remedies apply. Refund, challenge and recourse are contractual; a final rail transfer is never assumed to be reversible.
